# Contributing

Thanks for improving Product Manager Toolkit.

## Principles
- Keep each Skill focused on a distinct job.
- Ground outputs in source evidence and preserve traceability.
- Never invent missing rules, thresholds, policies, defaults, or expected behavior.
- Label facts, interpretations, assumptions, unknowns, conflicts, and proposals.
- Keep platform-specific instructions in `integrations/` where possible.
- Use synthetic examples and avoid unnecessary sensitive data.

## Skill checklist
Each Skill should state its purpose, inputs, process, expected outputs, quality checks, guardrails, and definition of done.

Before contributing, update catalog/docs, check internal links, add evaluations for important behavior changes, and review third-party licensing.
