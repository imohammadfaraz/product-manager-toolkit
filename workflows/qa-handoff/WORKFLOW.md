# QA Handoff Workflow

1. Collect approved requirements, criteria, Gherkin, test data, dependencies, and risks.
2. Review scenario quality and source traceability.
3. Review data setup, boundaries, permissions, states, environments, and observability.
4. Identify criteria blocked by unspecified behavior.
5. Separate manual test needs from automation candidates.
6. Prepare a handoff with open decisions and readiness caveats.

A readiness review is not test execution or stakeholder approval.
