# PRD to Acceptance Criteria

1. PRD Intake & Normalizer: inventory sources and normalize the requirements.
2. PRD Analyzer: identify ambiguity, conflicts, omissions, and untestable statements.
3. Requirement Decomposer: split broad requirements into traceable units.
4. Clarification & Decision Builder: record questions and decisions needed.
5. User Story Generator: create stories where useful.
6. Acceptance Criteria Generator: draft observable criteria from sources and approved decisions.
7. Business Rules & Decision Table Builder: structure conditional logic where relevant.
8. PRD-to-Gherkin Converter: map requirements and criteria to Gherkin.
9. Scenario Coverage Planner and Edge Case Generator: inspect coverage.
10. Gherkin Quality Reviewer and Traceability Matrix Builder: review quality and mapping.
11. Coverage Gap Analyzer: identify gaps and orphans.
12. QA Handoff: summarize readiness and blockers.

Track each step as not started, completed, blocked, or skipped, with evidence and rationale. This guide does not automatically execute Skills. Never silently invent undefined behavior.
