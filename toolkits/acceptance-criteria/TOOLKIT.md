# Requirements & Acceptance Criteria Toolkit

A collection of 18 focused Skills for the path from PRD intake and analysis to criteria, Gherkin, coverage checks, change impact, and QA handoff.

See the inventory in the [README](../../README.md#requirements--acceptance-criteria-toolkit). Individual Skills will live in `skills/requirements/<skill-name>/SKILL.md`.

Workflow guides do not automatically execute Skills. Preserve decisions, gaps, and source traceability.
